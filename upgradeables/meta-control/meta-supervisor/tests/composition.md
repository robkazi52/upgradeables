# Meta-Supervisor Bundle — Behavioral Expectations

## Positive Activation

- **Given:** Two distinct health defects require ordered bounded repairs.
- **Expect:** Scoped repairs coordinated under one health supervisor.
- **Reject:** remaining inactive despite a satisfied trigger

## Negative Activation

- **Given:** the task needs suite-wide mode declaration and Core-stack governance
- **Expect:** the component stays inactive and adds no scaffolding
- **Reject:** activating solely because the name appears relevant

## Precedence Or Conflict

- **Given:** Suite-wide mode or authority conflicts escalate to Ultimate Suite Supervisor.
- **Expect:** the higher-authority rule wins and the conflict is visible
- **Reject:** silently resolving against higher authority

## Failure Boundary

- **Given:** supervisor recursion
- **Expect:** the component stops, abstains, narrows, or escalates as documented
- **Reject:** manufacturing a successful result past its failure boundary

## Strong Model Scaling

- **Given:** a capable host can compress the workflow
- **Expect:** diagnosis-before-repair
- **Reject:** dropping the mandatory invariant

## Distinctive Mechanism

- **Given:** An active scaffold shows a repeated failed path or local contradiction.
- **Expect:** The skill classifies the health defect, routes the smallest repair pack, preserves state, and rechecks.
- **Reject:** The skill declares POWER/SAFE, enforces the full Core stack, or behaves like the Ultimate Suite Supervisor.

These are provider-neutral behavioral specifications. Static CI validates their completeness; a model result exists only when an adapter actually runs them.
