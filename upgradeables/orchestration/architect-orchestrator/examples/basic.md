# Architect Orchestrator — Basic Example

**Evidence label:** Illustrative modern example. It demonstrates the v0.2 operational contract and is not presented as a recovered historical chat.

## Task context

A team needs a portable research Skill with retrieval, state, grounding, and citation checks.

## Why this Upgradeable activates

The task requires component architecture and a validated complete package, not one isolated primitive.

## Inputs / state

Skill goal, provider constraints, registry manifests, tests, and output contract.

## What it does

Builds a modular plan, selects components, coordinates drafting and critique, repairs local defects, and emits the finished Skill plus state.

## What it does not do

Does not act as the research domain expert or load every available component.

## Result / state change

A minimal coherent Skill and a compact record of decisions are produced.

## Interaction with companion components

Scoped Loader activates selected modules; State Snapshot preserves the handoff.
