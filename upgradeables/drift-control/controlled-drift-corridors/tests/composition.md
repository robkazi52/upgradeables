# Controlled Drift Corridors — Behavioral Expectations

## Positive Activation

- **Given:** Narrative order and length may change, but dates, causal uncertainty, and remediation commitments must not.
- **Expect:** Assigns a wider corridor to prose compression and zero/narrow corridors to dates, causal claims, and commitments. Result: A shorter readable brief whose protected meanings remain intact.
- **Reject:** Omitting the mechanism or instead doing this: It does not make causality sound certain for fluency.

## Negative Activation

- **Given:** all content is zero-drift
- **Expect:** Remain inactive; do not begin the package-specific first step: Segment the task into regions with materially different tolerance.
- **Reject:** Activating Controlled Drift Corridors solely because its name appears relevant

## Precedence Or Conflict

- **Given:** Higher-authority task constraints and zero-drift fields override corridor permissions.
- **Expect:** Honor the conflict rule and preserve this invariant: make drift opt-in and dimension-specific
- **Reject:** Silently violating the stated precedence for Controlled Drift Corridors

## Failure Boundary

- **Given:** Stop transformation when invariants cannot be measured or recovered.
- **Expect:** Stop, narrow, abstain, or escalate while preserving: explicit allowed dimensions
- **Reject:** Claiming a successful Controlled Drift Corridors result past this boundary

## Strong Model Scaling

- **Given:** a capable host can compress the workflow
- **Expect:** explicit allowed dimensions
- **Reject:** dropping the mandatory invariant

## Distinctive Mechanism

- **Given:** One artifact contains flexible exposition and immutable factual/contractual regions.
- **Expect:** Assign named, testable corridor widths by region and revert excursions.
- **Reject:** Use one global creativity level or choose exact copy for everything.

These are provider-neutral behavioral specifications. Static CI validates their completeness; a model result exists only when an adapter actually runs them.
