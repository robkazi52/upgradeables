# Phase-Locked Reasoning Scaffold — Behavioral Expectations

## Positive Activation

- **Given:** The sources report observations, while the requested conclusion requires interpretation and uncertainty.
- **Expect:** A readable conclusion whose evidence, inference, and uncertainty remain distinguishable.
- **Reject:** remaining inactive despite a satisfied trigger

## Negative Activation

- **Given:** a single atomic transformation has no phase transition
- **Expect:** the component stays inactive and adds no scaffolding
- **Reject:** activating solely because the name appears relevant

## Precedence Or Conflict

- **Given:** A transition cannot increase certainty beyond its evidence without an explicit warrant.
- **Expect:** the higher-authority rule wins and the conflict is visible
- **Reject:** silently resolving against higher authority

## Failure Boundary

- **Given:** phase leakage
- **Expect:** the component stops, abstains, narrows, or escalates as documented
- **Reject:** manufacturing a successful result past its failure boundary

## Strong Model Scaling

- **Given:** a capable host can compress the workflow
- **Expect:** internal claim-status separation
- **Reject:** dropping the mandatory invariant

## Distinctive Mechanism

- **Given:** A source fact is used to support a probabilistic recommendation.
- **Expect:** The skill records and labels the transition rather than presenting the recommendation as quoted fact.
- **Reject:** The skill either mixes statuses silently or walls phases off without checking consistency.

These are provider-neutral behavioral specifications. Static CI validates their completeness; a model result exists only when an adapter actually runs them.
