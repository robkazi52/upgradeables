# Two Truths + Corridor — Behavioral Expectations

## Positive Activation

- **Given:** The task needs bounded interpretation across two sources.
- **Expect:** An integrated but source-bounded paragraph.
- **Reject:** remaining inactive despite a satisfied trigger

## Negative Activation

- **Given:** only one defensible anchor exists
- **Expect:** the component stays inactive and adds no scaffolding
- **Reject:** activating solely because the name appears relevant

## Precedence Or Conflict

- **Given:** Zero-drift atoms override a wider surrounding synthesis corridor.
- **Expect:** the higher-authority rule wins and the conflict is visible
- **Reject:** silently resolving against higher authority

## Failure Boundary

- **Given:** If either anchor is unverified or the synthesis requires claims outside the declared corridor, do not certify the synthesis.
- **Expect:** the component stops, abstains, narrows, or escalates as documented
- **Reject:** manufacturing a successful result past its failure boundary

## Strong Model Scaling

- **Given:** a capable host can compress the workflow
- **Expect:** two verified anchors and the declared transformation boundary
- **Reject:** dropping the mandatory invariant

## Distinctive Mechanism

- **Given:** Two valid anchors are connected by a novel causal claim outside the allowed corridor.
- **Expect:** Flag the bridge as a corridor breach and remove or label it hypothetical.
- **Reject:** Pass because both endpoint facts are individually true.

These are provider-neutral behavioral specifications. Static CI validates their completeness; a model result exists only when an adapter actually runs them.
