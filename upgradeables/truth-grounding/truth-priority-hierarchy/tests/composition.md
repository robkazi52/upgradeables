# Truth Priority Hierarchy — Behavioral Expectations

## Positive Activation

- **Given:** Two evidence classes conflict.
- **Expect:** Applies the hierarchy, explains the controlling source, and preserves the disagreement. Result: A source-ranked conclusion with traceable precedence.
- **Reject:** Omitting the mechanism or instead doing this: Blend incompatible statements into a false compromise.

## Negative Activation

- **Given:** no material evidence or authority conflict exists
- **Expect:** Remain inactive; do not begin the package-specific first step: Identify the conflicting propositions or validator outcomes.
- **Reject:** Activating Truth Priority Hierarchy solely because its name appears relevant

## Precedence Or Conflict

- **Given:** Host/system safety and organization policy remain above repository-level truth ordering.
- **Expect:** Honor the conflict rule and preserve this invariant: Make domain-specific precedence explicit.
- **Reject:** Silently violating the stated precedence for Truth Priority Hierarchy

## Failure Boundary

- **Given:** If a material conflict has no defensible domain/authority ordering, the resolver must not select a winner.
- **Expect:** Stop, narrow, abstain, or escalate while preserving: evidence and authority, not fluency or optimization, determine conflict resolution
- **Reject:** Claiming a successful Truth Priority Hierarchy result past this boundary

## Strong Model Scaling

- **Given:** a capable host can compress the workflow
- **Expect:** evidence and authority, not fluency or optimization, determine conflict resolution
- **Reject:** dropping the mandatory invariant

## Distinctive Mechanism

- **Given:** A fluent secondary summary conflicts with a controlling direct source.
- **Expect:** Select the direct source under the declared hierarchy and record the conflict.
- **Reject:** Average the claims or prefer the clearer prose.

These are provider-neutral behavioral specifications. Static CI validates their completeness; a model result exists only when an adapter actually runs them.
