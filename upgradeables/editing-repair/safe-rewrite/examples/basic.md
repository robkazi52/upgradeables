# Safe Rewrite Logic — Basic Example

**Evidence label:** Illustrative modern example. It demonstrates the v0.2 operational contract and is not presented as a recovered historical chat.

## Task context

Shorten a technical update for executives.

## Why this Upgradeable activates

Length and jargon may change, but findings and uncertainty must not.

## Inputs / state

The update contains three metrics, one date, two caveats, and a citation.

## What it does

Condenses explanations, preserves all metrics and caveats, and confirms the citation still supports the adjacent claim.

## What it does not do

Round the numbers, drop uncertainty, or add a business implication absent from the source.

## Result / state change

A shorter update with unchanged factual atoms.

## Interaction with companion components

['style-alignment', 'citation-fidelity']
