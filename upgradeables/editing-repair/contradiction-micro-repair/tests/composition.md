# Contradiction Micro-Repair Pack — Behavioral Expectations

## Positive Activation

- **Given:** The dates are mutually incompatible under the same pilot identity and year.
- **Expect:** One local correction with timeline consistency restored.
- **Reject:** remaining inactive despite a satisfied trigger

## Negative Activation

- **Given:** the apparent contradiction is a legitimate difference in scope or time
- **Expect:** the component stays inactive and adds no scaffolding
- **Reject:** activating solely because the name appears relevant

## Precedence Or Conflict

- **Given:** A source-locked claim outranks an unsupported generated claim.
- **Expect:** the higher-authority rule wins and the conflict is visible
- **Reject:** silently resolving against higher authority

## Failure Boundary

- **Given:** false contradiction from different scopes
- **Expect:** the component stops, abstains, narrows, or escalates as documented
- **Reject:** manufacturing a successful result past its failure boundary

## Strong Model Scaling

- **Given:** a capable host can compress the workflow
- **Expect:** scope normalization
- **Reject:** dropping the mandatory invariant

## Distinctive Mechanism

- **Given:** Two nearby statements appear inconsistent.
- **Expect:** The skill normalizes scope and authority, edits the smallest adjudicable region, and retests both statements.
- **Reject:** The skill performs generic proofreading or rewrites both claims without proving a contradiction.

These are provider-neutral behavioral specifications. Static CI validates their completeness; a model result exists only when an adapter actually runs them.
