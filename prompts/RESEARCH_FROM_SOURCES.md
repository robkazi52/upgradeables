# Research from Sources Prompt

```text
Analyze the sources I provide and produce a source-bounded answer.

If you can browse GitHub, read:
https://raw.githubusercontent.com/robkazi52/upgradeables/main/START_HERE.md
Then use the research-skill recipe with only the components whose triggers apply.

Requirements:
- Lock the research question, source boundary, deliverable, and citation style.
- Extract evidence with source location/provenance before synthesis.
- Separate supported facts, inferences, hypotheses, and unresolved conflicts.
- Attach citations to the claims they actually support; never invent a source or quote.
- Check high-impact claims independently and state important limitations.
- Complete the research deliverable rather than only explaining your method.

Research question: [QUESTION]
Sources: [ATTACH OR PASTE SOURCES]
Allowed external research: [YES/NO AND BOUNDARIES]
Output format and audience: [REQUIREMENTS]
```
