## Recovered or normalized purpose

Protect factual extraction and reporting tasks from unsolicited counterfactual elaboration.

## Operational mechanism

After a factual-only mode is locked, inspect the candidate for propositions introduced through if, might-have, imagined, alternative-history, or unstated causal premises. Remove those propositions unless they are explicitly reported as source content; preserve ordinary uncertainty statements and supported inference rather than suppressing all modal language.

## Trigger and task use

Triggers: factual output could be contaminated by hypothetical content. Best-fit tasks: record extraction, source-faithful summarization, incident reporting, citation-bound authoring.

## Interactions and failure boundary

Companions: counterfactual-integrity, mode-lock-in. Failure boundary: If factual and counterfactual propositions cannot be distinguished reliably, request review rather than deleting uncertain content wholesale..
