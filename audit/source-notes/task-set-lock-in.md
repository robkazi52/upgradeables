## Recovered or normalized purpose

Prevent scope substitution and goal drift during execution.

## Operational mechanism

Convert the clarified request into a compact task-set contract: primary objective, required outputs, quality gates, constraints, non-goals, dependencies, and change authority. Check each planned action and final artifact against it; update only through an explicit, versioned scope-change decision.

## Trigger and task use

Triggers: multi-step work begins or scope changes. Best-fit tasks: multi-step builds, contracted deliverables, long research, tasks with exclusions.

## Interactions and failure boundary

Companions: clarification-gateway, mode-lock-in, stateblock. Failure boundary: Do not claim completion when a required artifact or quality gate lacks evidence.; Unlock and clarify when task identity changes materially..
